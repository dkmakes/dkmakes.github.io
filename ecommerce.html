Samsung Card · E-commerce · 2016

Members' Shopping Mall

A UX-led revamp of Samsung Card's members-only online shopping mall, with a clear renewal direction across information structure, merchandising, and the path to purchase.

CompanySamsung Card
Role / TypeE-commerce web service
Year2016
The challenge

A members' mall that wasn't converting its audience.

Samsung Card's members-only shopping mall had a built-in, loyal audience, but an ageing structure and unclear merchandising meant that audience wasn't converting into buyers.

The revamp set a clear renewal direction: reorganise the information structure, sharpen merchandising, and smooth the path from browsing to purchase.

Approach

Rebuild around the path to purchase.

1

Structure

Reorganise categories and hierarchy so products are easy to find.

2

Merchandising

Surface the right offers to a members' audience more clearly.

3

Checkout path

Smooth the steps from browse to buy to reduce drop-off.

The product

A clearer members' shopping experience.

The renewed mall gives Samsung Card members a cleaner, more shoppable experience aligned to how they actually browse and buy.

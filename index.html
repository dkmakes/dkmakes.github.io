Turning a shifting metric into a governed AI prompt

Most AI design tools wait for a prompt. I built a system that starts a step earlier: it watches business metrics, walks the designer through UX-theory causes when one slips, and turns the chosen direction into a prompt scoped to design-system tokens, so every AI-generated screen stays consistent.
